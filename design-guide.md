# AZ Pharmacy Website — Design Guide

## Website purpose

A simple informational website for a local pharmacy. It introduces the business, showcases selected products and categories, explains available services, and helps visitors call, message, or find the store.

This is **not** an e-commerce website or pharmacy management system.

## Recommended pages

1. Home
2. About Us
3. Products
4. Services
5. Contact Us

## Visual direction

- **Primary:** Medical blue
- **Secondary:** Soft green
- **Background:** White and very light gray
- **Text:** Dark navy or charcoal
- **Style:** Clean, welcoming, modern, trustworthy
- **Typography:** Clear sans-serif font
- **Images:** Real pharmacy/store/team photos where possible; high-quality product category imagery otherwise

## Homepage section order

1. Header with logo, navigation, Call Now, and WhatsApp
2. Hero headline, short introduction, and calls to action
3. About AZ Pharmacy preview
4. Product categories and selected featured products
5. Services preview
6. Why choose AZ Pharmacy
7. Contact call-to-action
8. Customer reviews only after genuine reviews are available
9. Location, opening hours, and map
10. Footer

## Functional requirements

- Responsive on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Click-to-call phone link
- WhatsApp link
- Google Maps directions link or embed
- Contact form with basic spam protection
- Accessible color contrast and keyboard navigation
- Optimized images and fast page loading
- Page titles and descriptions for local search visibility

## Keep it simple

No customer accounts, shopping cart, checkout, payment gateway, inventory database, stock dashboard, or staff management features are needed for this first website.

## Content accuracy

- Replace all placeholders with verified details.
- Do not invent reviews, addresses, phone numbers, opening hours, or services.
- Do not claim that a product is in stock unless availability is verified.
- Use accurate product descriptions and avoid unsupported medical claims.
- Publish only services the pharmacy actually provides and is authorized to offer.
